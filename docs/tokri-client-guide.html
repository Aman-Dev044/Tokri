Tokri — Platform Guide

Every role, every menu, and how one order travels through all of them.

What is Tokri?

Tokri is a quick-commerce grocery platform, in the style of Blinkit, built for many local stores at once. Nearby kirana, dairy and bakery stores list their products. Customers order from the store closest to them, and a rider delivers in about 20–30 minutes. The Tokri team watches everything from one console and pays each store its share every settlement cycle.

The life of one order

This is the main flow of the whole platform. Every role touches it at some point. Colours show who does each step.

CustomerPicks a nearby store, fills the cart, checks out (COD or online).
SystemRe-checks price and stock, reserves the stock, creates the order and a 4-digit delivery OTP.
VendorGets the new order on the Orders board and accepts it (or rejects it with a reason).
VendorPacks the items, then marks the order "Ready for pickup".
SystemOffers the job to the nearest free rider. Each offer lasts 45 seconds, and if it isn't taken the next rider gets it.
RiderAccepts the job. A small pick fee is taken from the rider's wallet.
RiderPicks up at the store, then starts "Out for delivery". The customer sees the rider live.
CustomerTells the rider the 4-digit OTP at the door.
RiderTypes the OTP. The order is Delivered and any COD cash has been collected.
Super AdminGenerates the settlement and pays the store its share (sales − commission − refunds).
Admin watches the whole journey live on the Live board. They can step in at any point: assign a rider by hand in Dispatch, cancel an order, or open an Issue.

Who earns what

All money is stored in paise, so there are no rounding errors. Every rupee movement is written to a ledger that cannot be edited.

Customer pays

Items + GST + delivery fee − coupon.
Delivery fee: ₹29
Free above ₹499
(both changeable in Settings)

Store receives

Its item sales minus Tokri's commission, minus refunds and minus its own store coupons.
Net = Gross − Commission − Refunds
Default commission: 12%

Tokri keeps

The commission on every delivered order, plus the delivery fee. Platform-wide coupons are paid by Tokri.
Commission % is set per store.
A change applies to new orders only.
Riders: each job pays ₹20 + ₹5 per km, shown on the rider's earnings statement. To accept a job, the rider pays a small pick fee (₹25–₹50) from a prepaid wallet. The fee is refunded if the job is taken away. COD cash the rider collects is shown as owed to Tokri, not as income.

What runs automatically

These jobs run on their own inside the server, with no human needed.

JobHow oftenWhat it does
Rider assignmentevery 5 sFinds "Ready" orders with no rider and offers them to the nearest free rider within the store's delivery radius. Each offer lasts 45 s. After 3 misses it alerts the store and the admin team.
Rider heartbeatevery 30 sThe rider app checks in every 30 seconds. After about 65 seconds of silence the rider is marked offline, and a job not yet picked up is offered to someone else.
Unpaid order clean-upevery 1 minAn online order not paid within 15 minutes is cancelled and its stock goes back on the shelf.
Payment checkevery 15 minSafety net: re-checks any payment stuck as "pending" with the payment gateway.
Notificationsevery 5 sSends queued emails, SMS and push notifications, and retries if a provider is down.
Live updates: order status, new orders, job offers, rider location, wallet balance and KYC status all reach the screen instantly, with no refresh needed.

  • Passwords are shared separately. They are never written in this document.
  • For safety, login is limited to 5 tries per minute. A session stays signed in for 7 days.
  • Customers sign up themselves with a phone OTP. Stores and riders apply from the same sign-up page and can work once the Tokri team approves them.
  • Hiding a menu is not the security. Every request is checked on the server against the person's role.

Before go-live — services to connect

The code for these is already built. Each one only needs the account keys to be added to the server.

  • Razorpay (online payments): until connected, online checkout and rider wallet top-ups show "not available yet". Cash on delivery works today.
  • Google Maps billing: until it's switched on, live tracking maps show a placeholder. Distance, ETA and payouts still work.
  • File storage (Amazon S3): needed for product photos, KYC document photos and delivery photo proof.
  • SMS / Email / Push providers: needed to send the customer sign-up OTP and notifications.
  • Domain + HTTPS: recommended before real customers use it, for the browser padlock and for the phone camera and location to work reliably.
